HOW THE MODEL WORKS

Build the skill first. Then build the system.

FHG combines practical digital work with community-based business building. The purpose is to help people create active income from useful skills while learning how to build relationships, leadership and an organization.

1

Choose a practical skill

Website design, graphics, email marketing, lead generation, digital marketing, project support and other services businesses already buy.

2

Practice until you can solve a real problem

Training only matters when you can turn it into work. Build samples, learn tools and improve through feedback.

3

Learn client acquisition

Position your offer, research prospects, communicate professionally, deliver and improve your reputation.

4

Build people, not just transactions

The network side teaches product knowledge, relationship building, mentorship and team development under the applicable company plan.

WHY FHG EXISTS

A practical response to a real problem: people needed skills and a way to fund progress.

Public FHG materials describe the organization as beginning in Ilesa, Osun State, after founder Jagunmolu AbdulFatai saw that many people struggled to remain active in traditional network marketing because they lacked income. The solution was to combine digital-skills training with the business-building side.

That idea remains simple: do not wait for money to appear. Learn something useful, become better at solving problems, and use what you build to create more options.
