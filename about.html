ABOUT FHG'T

A community built around learning, earning from skills, and growing through people.

FHG'T brings together digital-skills development, freelancing and network marketing. It is not a shortcut around work. It is a structure designed to give committed people a place to learn, practice and build.

THE BEGINNING

Why it got started

Public FHG history traces the model to Ilesa, Osun State, around 2015–2016. Founder Jagunmolu AbdulFatai began with small training sessions and later combined digital freelancing with the network-marketing model.

The problem was practical: many people wanted to build the business side but did not have stable income to support themselves or remain consistent. Adding digital skills created another route: learn a service, find clients, earn actively, and build the business side with less pressure.

THE IDEA

Learn → Earn → Build → Grow

Learn a useful skill. Use it to solve real problems. Build relationships and a team. Grow your competence, leadership and options over time.

WHAT FHG DOES

Two connected tracks

A

Digital skills & freelancing

Members can learn practical services such as website design, graphics, lead generation, email marketing, digital marketing, project support and related skills. The aim is not merely to “learn a course” but to become capable enough to solve a client problem.

B

Network marketing & team building

Members can also learn how a product-based network-marketing business works, including product knowledge, communication, mentorship, customer relationships and team development. Any commissions depend on the applicable company plan and actual qualifying activity.

WHO IT HAS HELPED

People at different starting points

Across public FHG pages and member profiles, you can find students, freelancers, developers, designers, marketers, workers and team builders. Some use the community to learn their first digital skill; others already have skills and need help turning them into offers, outreach and paid work.

Public profiles also show FHG-associated people working in web development, UI/UX, e-commerce, writing and other digital services. That does not mean everybody reaches the same outcome. It shows the skills path is real and independently visible.

WHAT WE DO NOT PROMISE
We do not promise that joining automatically makes you rich, that every member earns the same amount, or that results happen quickly.

Results depend on ability, practice, consistency, sales and communication skills, market conditions, client acquisition and the time you put in.

ABOUT CRITICISM

Serious questions deserve serious answers.

Network marketing attracts criticism, and some criticism comes from real bad experiences with individual teams or misleading promises. We do not dismiss that. We invite people to verify the model, understand obligations, ask where money comes from, inspect public profiles, and decide with clear eyes.

Ask questions

You should understand the training, product/business responsibilities, compensation structure and the role of your sponsor or team.

Check evidence

Look at public FHG pages, social profiles, freelancer portfolios and real member work rather than relying only on motivational claims.

Judge the fit

If you want guaranteed salary, instant returns or a passive investment, this is not that. If you want a learn-and-build process, then explore it further.
