FAQ

Ask before you join.

You should understand the process before committing your time. These answers are written to be clear, not to pressure you.

What exactly is FHG'T?

Faitheroic Global Team is a community that combines digital-skill development and freelancing with network-marketing/business-building training. The digital side focuses on learning services and finding clients. The network side focuses on products, relationships, mentorship and team development.

Do I need a skill before I join?

No. Beginners can start by choosing one skill and learning it. If you already have a skill, the bigger need may be positioning, client acquisition, communication, pricing and delivery.

Is this a job?

No. It is not a fixed-salary job. Freelancing income depends on getting and serving clients. Network-marketing commissions depend on actual qualifying activity under the relevant compensation plan.

How much will I make?

There is no guaranteed amount. Do not join based on somebody else’s screenshot or income story. Results vary based on skill level, consistency, market demand, communication, client acquisition, team activity and other factors.

Is it fast money?

No. If your only reason for joining is to make money immediately, this may frustrate you. Learning a skill, building samples, finding clients and becoming good enough to earn consistently can take time.

Can students do it?

Yes, many people participate while studying, but you still need a realistic schedule. School responsibilities should not be ignored.

Can workers do it part-time?

Often yes. The amount you can do depends on your schedule, the skill you choose, client workload and team commitments.

Why combine freelancing with network marketing?

The FHG explanation is that active income from digital work can reduce the pressure to depend only on network-marketing activity. It also gives members a standalone marketable skill.

What about criticism of network marketing?

Criticism exists for many reasons, including exaggerated recruitment claims, bad experiences and poor practices by individuals or teams. You should ask direct questions, understand costs and obligations, inspect the compensation structure and refuse any promise that sounds like guaranteed wealth.

Is this the official FHG or NeoLife website?

No. This is an independent team information and recruitment site. It is not the official corporate website of NeoLife and should not be read as a corporate guarantee or official earnings claim.

STILL UNSURE?

Ask directly. No pressure.

You can ask about training, time commitment, how freelancing works, how the network side works, or what you need to start.
